03 · ESP32-WROOM playback setup

SD-card WAV clips16 kHz · mono · 16-bit
SD card folders

/sd/billi_voice/ta/hello.wav
/sd/billi_voice/si/hello.wav
  1. Record and download the phrase clips above.
  2. Copy Tamil WAV files to /billi_voice/ta/ and Sinhala to /billi_voice/si/.
  3. Mount your SD card at /sd.
  4. Configure I2S TX: 16 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM.
  5. Attach SDWavSpeaker and call voice.speak_clip("hello").
ESP32 connection: not connected. This page prepares audio files; it does not directly control the board.

Use your confirmed BILLI pin map and a compatible I2S DAC/amplifier. Pin numbers are deliberately not guessed.
